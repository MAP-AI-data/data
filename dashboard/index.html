What the numbers mean

AI-active firm
A firm whose website, read by a large language model, shows that it uses, develops or deploys AI. MAP-AI measures adoption that has happened, not exposure to AI.
Cumulative
Once a firm is classified as AI-active, it counts in every later year. Roles and technology types work the same way.
Classified firms
Firms that could be matched to classified website content in a year: the denominator of every AI-active share.
Roles and technology types
Given only to AI-active firms, and a firm can have several, so the shares of AI-active firms add up to more than 100%.
All four countries
For the countries together, this dashboard adds up AI-active firms (or firms in a category) across Belgium, France, Germany and Luxembourg and divides by the summed denominator. For industries it uses the repository’s own pooled rows.
Withheld shares
A share is withheld when its denominator has fewer than — firms. Here those show as hatched cells or missing dots.

Source and coverage

  • Source: firm websites from Common Crawl, classified with the MAP-AI method.
  • Countries: Belgium, France, Germany and Luxembourg.
  • Years: — to —.
  • Regions: Eurostat NUTS 2024, levels 1 to 3.
  • Industries: NACE Rev. 2 sections and divisions, and NAICS 2022 sectors.

The repository also holds finer industries (NACE groups and classes, NAICS industry groups and industries) and roles and technology types by region, which this dashboard leaves out. Industry and region names were written out from the published classifications rather than downloaded: check them against Eurostat and the US Census Bureau before citing them.
